World refinement · September 2026

Places shaped by the people who came before.

Regional homes, carved heroes and guardian angels bring more character to the descent. Clouds gather around the highest stages, then thin as Caelum travels toward humanity.

Homes along the mountain.

21 Blender house designs across seven regional styles. Revised plots give 54 homes grounded terraces, supported lamps and clear entrance paths.

Memories carved in stone.

Seven hero sculptures and three winged guardian designs, with regional relics, draped clothing and layered feathers.

A clearer descent.

Upper-stage cloud banks, fitted supports for shelters and civic structures, and improved camera clearance beside shrine columns and gate arches.

A note from the creator

Why I’m building this game.

For me, it starts with the endless shackles of work handed down from above. I wanted to be different. I wanted to show my own vision, and this game is how I’m doing that.

Defining what’s missing in our lives, and what it means to become human, is what I wanted to explore through DOWNFALL. I relate that feeling to Caelum’s journey from Heaven toward humanity.

I also follow the history of old heroes and bring their stories into the world I’m building. God bless them and their contributions. In DOWNFALL, I imagine heroes who are already shackled above, still bound to duty. Their stories are part of how I express that same feeling of wanting a different path.

I just relate everything here: the work, the old heroes, the shackles, and the wish to become human. This is what I wanted to show through this game. My own vision.

— Retro B.O.T.

The price of eternity

Heaven kept their glory.
It took their memories.

You are Caelum, Heaven’s former Grand Archivist. You choose to surrender immortality for a human life. The road toward Earth winds down a vast mountain, through ruined sanctuaries, steep passes, and the shelters left by those who came before.

The champions guarding your path were once human, too. Recover the memories Heaven erased, confront the lives hidden beneath their armor, and discover why a finite life is worth the descent.

On the way down

Inside the playable prototype
01 / THE WORLD

Follow the mountain.

Descend winding paths, cross deep gorges, and find quiet refuges in a connected world shaped by changing elevations.

Break their bindings.
