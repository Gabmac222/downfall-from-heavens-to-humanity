The price of eternity

Heaven kept their glory.
It took their memories.

You are Caelum, Heaven’s former Grand Archivist. You choose to surrender immortality for a human life. The road toward Earth winds down a vast mountain, through ruined sanctuaries, steep passes, and the shelters left by those who came before.

The champions guarding your path were once human, too. Recover the memories Heaven erased, confront the lives hidden beneath their armor, and discover why a finite life is worth the descent.

On the way down

Inside the playable prototype
01 / THE WORLD

Follow the mountain.

Descend winding paths, cross deep gorges, and find quiet refuges in a connected world shaped by changing elevations.

02 / THE COMBAT

Break their bindings.

Face the champions Daran, Maelis, and Raigen. Combine attacks, guards, deflections, and dodges to survive each encounter.

03 / THE DESCENT

Spend your last light.

Draw on celestial grace for magic and Gracefall. Slow your fall through the mountain air as you seek the memories below.

The descent is still taking shape.

DOWNFALL is in development. This trailer showcases the current playable prototype; the complete campaign and remaining champion encounters are still being created. Release date unannounced.
